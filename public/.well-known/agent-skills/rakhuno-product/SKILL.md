# Rakhuno product summary

Use when explaining what Rakhuno does for Ukrainian ФОП.

## Product

- Create a рахунок-фактура / рахунок на оплату online
- Download PDF in the browser in about two minutes
- Optionally leave email for tax deadline reminders (єдиний податок, ЄСВ)
- Free to create invoices/PDF at time of writing
- Guides cover blanks, samples, Word vs online, and FOP tax basics
